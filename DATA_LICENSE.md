# Derived data

The files in `data/manifests/` and `data/splits/` (car ids, car geometry tables, splits, label draws and folds) are derived from DrivAerNet++
(Elrefaie, Morar, Dai, Ahmed, NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks), which is released under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0
International license (CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). They are shared under the same license: non-commercial
use only, with attribution to DrivAerNet++ and to this repository. No mesh, pressure field, cache or model checkpoint of the dataset is included.
